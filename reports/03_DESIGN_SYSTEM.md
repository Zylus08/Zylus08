# Visual language

Source of truth: `config/design_tokens.json`. 8 px spacing unit; 28 px module inset; 16 px gaps. Wide canvas 960, module 460, mobile 480. Opaque obsidian panels, off-white editorial lettering, muted slate annotations. Cyan systems; lavender intelligence; warm orange quant. Cobalt supports tensor motifs. Text colors are intentionally brighter than the initial brief for small-size contrast.

Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif heavy display typography; Consolas/Liberation Mono/monospace only for IDs, dates and instrumentation. No external fonts. Display lettering is 54–210 px depending on composition; module body 17–20 px; labels 13–15 px. At 375 px, modules scale to roughly 75% and essential descriptions remain readable in Markdown.

Rules: crisp corners; 1 px separators; no shadows on cards; data shadows only on isometric buildings. Structural lines are subdued and carry no essential meaning. Quant curves are explicitly mathematical illustrations, never returns. Technology logos retain their original geometry with a consistent 28 px box. No proficiency bars. Axis and height meanings accompany every data visualization.
