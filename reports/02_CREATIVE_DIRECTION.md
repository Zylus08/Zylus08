# M1 · An editorial observatory

Three deterministic concept SVGs and browser PNGs are in `concepts/`.

1. Mission Control: excellent structure, but a full-canvas grid competes with identity.
2. Digital Brutalism: strongest recognition and hierarchy; full cyan is too loud for an entire profile.
3. Computational Renaissance: strongest mathematical personality; requires more navigational structure.

Selected hybrid: B's enormous grotesk wordmark, C's actual Lorenz trajectories and A's restrained data labels. Dark scientific plates alternate with open Markdown, compact research indexes and pale editorial accents. Cyan identifies systems, lavender discovery, orange quantitative work. No ornamental gauges or fake operational states. The Lorenz butterfly is the recurring signature.

Assets have opaque canvases and work in either GitHub theme. Small-screen picture alternatives recompose the hero and skyline, while fixed-intrinsic-width module images wrap naturally. Essential claims also appear as ordinary text/alt text.

Motion is deferred: GIF support is established by reference profiles, but continuous movement adds payload and distraction. No claim of tested live animation is made. Static mathematical artwork is the polished default, not a degraded placeholder.
